03 / What should a credit committee do?

Managerial implications are conditional recommendations, not assertions about the actual performance of any lender.

Risk interpretation

A falling aggregate debt ratio should not be taken as proof that every borrower has more capacity to pay. High floating-rate exposure can transmit interest-rate changes into debt service quickly. Borrower-level outcomes depend on income, debt size, amortisation, other liabilities and financial buffers.

Recommended next controls

Segment exposures by disposable-income buffer, debt service and refinancing profile; test parallel income/rate shocks; distinguish interest-only pressure from annuity payments; monitor arrears migration and performance by segment before recalibrating underwriting thresholds.

04 / Research discipline

Source audit trail and interpretation safeguards.

Official observations: SSB financial accounts for households (2021 H2 / 2023 / 2024) and SSB mortgage interest rate statistics (September and Q3 2026). Simulation: illustrative borrower with a fully amortising mortgage. Macro ratios and scenario outcomes are not statistically linked or calibrated to a common microdataset. No default probability or predictive accuracy is claimed.
